              For families & teachers

              Watch, sing, talk, play

              • 1

                Watch together

                Pause the song and ask: "How many?", "What comes next?", "How does she feel?" Watching together turns a video into a conversation.

              • 2

                Sing and move

                Every song has simple moves. Stand up, copy T4L and sing the chorus. Routine songs work best at the same moment every day.

              • 3

                Talk about it

                Ask one open question after the song. Let children answer by talking, pointing, signing or showing a picture card.

              • 4

                Play off-screen

                Turn the screen off and use the free printables, real objects, mazes or coloring pages to practise the same idea.

                  FAQ

                  Common questions

                  Who are the songs for?

                  Children aged about 3 to 8: preschool, Pre-K, kindergarten and early elementary. Teachers use them in class and families use them at home.

                  Are the videos free?

                  Yes. All Tune4Learn songs are free to watch on YouTube and to listen to on Spotify and Apple Music. The printables on this page are free too.

                  How can teachers use the songs?

                  As circle-time openers, transitions, brain breaks, introductions to a new theme, or the start of a full themed lesson. Routine songs are made to be sung at the same moment every day.

                  Are the videos safe for children?

                  The songs are age-appropriate and free of violence or scary images. They contain no product placement or sponsored messages.

                  How often are new songs released?

                  Regularly through the year, with a focus on classroom routines, kindergarten math and international days. Subscribe on YouTube or join the newsletter to hear about new songs.
